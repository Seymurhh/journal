Pieces in the studio's library

All the music is in the public domain (the composers died between 1750 and 1925).
The MIDI files were made from the free editions of the Mutopia Project (https://www.mutopiaproject.org)
with LilyPond 2.24; each edition's own licence is given below and still applies to the file made from it.

Changes made here: the tempo was set for the Rondo alla Turca (126), the Prelude in C (70), the Moonlight Sonata (54)
and the Gymnopédie (66), whose files had none of their own; the sustain pedal was added by a simple rule (change the
pedal when the bass note changes) to the Prelude in C, Moonlight Sonata, Nocturne, Clair de lune, Gymnopédie and
Träumerei, whose editions gave no pedal in their MIDI. The Gnossienne was entered by hand from its edition.

The files end in .smf (Standard MIDI File) instead of .mid: they are ordinary MIDI files, renamed because this site
never publishes .mid files, so that her own recordings cannot be published by accident.
